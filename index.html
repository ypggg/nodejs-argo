Our priorities

Why it matters

Four pillars of the European Green Deal — protecting the planet while building a fair and prosperous society.

🌍

Climate Action

Cutting greenhouse-gas emissions in line with the Paris Agreement — Europe has already reduced its emissions by more than a third since 1990.

⚡

Clean Energy

Scaling up wind, solar and other renewables so that 42.5 % of EU energy comes from renewable sources by 2030.

🦅

Biodiversity

Restoring ecosystems and protecting 30 % of Europe's land and seas, building on the Natura 2000 network.

♻️

Circular Economy

Designing waste out of the system: all EU packaging recyclable by 2030, and the most littered single-use plastics banned since 2021.

Policy in action

What the EU does

Three decades of pioneering environmental legislation.

1992

EU Ecolabel

Europe's environmental excellence label, helping consumers choose products and services with a lower impact throughout their life cycle.

2005

EU Emissions Trading System

The world's first major carbon market — putting a price on pollution and driving emissions down where they matter most.

2021

European Climate Law

The 2030 and 2050 climate targets become legally binding, making backtracking on the Green Deal impossible.

2021

Single-Use Plastics Directive

A ban on the ten plastic items most often found on Europe's beaches — from cutlery and straws to polystyrene food containers.

2024

Nature Restoration Law

Binding targets to restore damaged ecosystems — from wetlands and forests to rivers, pollinators and marine habitats.

Ongoing

Natura 2000

The world's largest coordinated network of protected areas, with more than 27,000 sites across the continent.

Every choice counts

Take action

450 million Europeans. 27 Member States. One shared commitment — and it starts with you.

1

♻️ Recycle right

Follow your municipality's colour-coded sorting system — harmonised EU waste rules make recycling easier everywhere.

2

🏷️ Choose the EU Ecolabel

Look for the flower logo and the EU organic logo when shopping — certified products meet strict environmental criteria.

3

🚆 Travel by rail

Europe's rail network connects hundreds of cities — trains emit up to 90 % less CO₂ per passenger than flying.

4

💡 Save energy at home

Insulate, switch off standby and choose efficient appliances — every kilowatt-hour saved supports REPowerEU.
